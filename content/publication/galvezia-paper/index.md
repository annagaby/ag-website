abstract: Galvezia leucantha subsp. leucantha is a critically endangered endemic plant of the Galápagos Islands that has suffered drastic population declines due to introduced herbivores and habitat degradation. By 2007, only six individuals remained on northern Isabela Island. This study evaluated ex situ propagation, seed viability and germination, seedling growth, flowering phenology, and transplant survival to support the recovery of the subspecies. From 2017 to 2024, 94 nursery-grown plants were outplanted and monitored. The work increased the wild adult population and successfully re-established a second population at a site where the species had previously been extirpated.
